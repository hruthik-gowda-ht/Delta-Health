Stock Advisory

Outbreak signals translated into specific supply recommendations for clinics and pharmacies.

How this works: when a clinic shows an active symptom spike, we check what conditions doctors have recently logged treatments for there and match it to a likely disease pattern, recommending disease-specific supplies. Network-wide, we also flag treatments being logged much more often this week than last — a proxy for rising demand, useful for restocking even at clinics that aren't spiking. These are directional recommendations, not exact order quantities.
